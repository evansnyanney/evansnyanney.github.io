ONGOING RESEARCH · OHIO UNIVERSITY

3D point-cloud registration and geometric analysis

Research focus. Aligning 3D measurements and characterizing geometric uncertainty for reverse engineering and manufacturing quality assessment.

My work. I am developing statistical methods for point-cloud registration and uncertainty characterization. Manuscripts in preparation address minimax rigid registration, point-cloud structurization, and distributional inference following registration.

I am also collaborating with Vitruvian Building on point-cloud methods for concrete printing, geometric measurement, and quality assessment.

Related publication. My 2025 first-author paper in Manufacturing Letters uses unsupervised learning to study process and design relationships in 3D point clouds.

SPRING 2025 · ACADEMIC PROJECT

MyValorAI: decision making in microRTS

My work. I extended the CoacAI agent with a tunable aggression parameter and Nash-inspired combat decision rules.

Results. The agent achieved an 80% win rate in a 100-game tournament, with approximately 12% fewer unit losses.

Mentor: Dr. Chang Liu

03

FALL 2023 · ACADEMIC PROJECT

Context-driven movie recommendations

My work. I investigated SVD++ and cosine similarity for hybrid movie recommendations using Netflix data.

Mentor: Prof. Hansheng Lei

04

SPRING 2023 · ACADEMIC PROJECT

Neural collaborative filtering

My work. I evaluated neural collaborative filtering on Amazon data to model implicit user–item interactions.

Results. I improved recommendation accuracy by tuning the negative sampling rate, embedding size, and network depth.

Mentor: Dr. Dongchul Kim
