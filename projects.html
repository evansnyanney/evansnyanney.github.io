01

ONGOING RESEARCH · OHIO UNIVERSITY

3D point-cloud registration and geometric analysis

Problem. Using 3D measurements for reverse engineering and manufacturing quality assessment requires alignment and a characterization of geometric uncertainty.

My work. I develop statistical methods for point-cloud registration and uncertainty characterization, with ongoing manuscripts on minimax rigid registration, point-cloud structurization, and distributional inference following registration. This work includes collaboration with Vitruvian Building on concrete printing and geometric quality assessment.

Related published work. My first-author 2025 Manufacturing Letters paper explores process and design relationships in point clouds using unsupervised learning. The registration manuscripts above remain in preparation.

SPRING 2025 · ACADEMIC PROJECT

MyValorAI: decision making in microRTS

My contribution. Extended the CoacAI agent with a tunable aggression parameter and Nash-inspired combat decision rules under the mentorship of Dr. Chang Liu.

Evaluation. In a 100-game tournament, the project achieved an 80% win rate with approximately 12% fewer unit losses, as reported in my CV.

FALL 2023 · ACADEMIC PROJECT

Context-driven movie recommendations

Investigated SVD++ and cosine similarity on Netflix data to improve movie recommendations. This project explored hybrid recommendation approaches under the mentorship of Prof. Hansheng Lei.

04

SPRING 2023 · ACADEMIC PROJECT

Neural collaborative filtering

Evaluated neural collaborative filtering on Amazon data to model implicit user–item interactions. I tuned the negative sampling rate, embedding size, and network depth to improve recommendation accuracy, under the mentorship of Dr. Dongchul Kim.
