RESEARCH

3D geometry, uncertainty, and statistical learning

My research focuses on 3D point-cloud registration, geometric uncertainty quantification, and manufacturing quality assessment. I am also developing statistical and machine learning methods for clinical EEG analysis.

01

CURRENT RESEARCH

3D point-cloud registration and inference

I am developing statistical methods for 3D point-cloud registration and geometric uncertainty characterization in reverse engineering and additive manufacturing quality assessment.

With Dr. Zhaohui Geng, I am preparing manuscripts on three topics:

  • Minimax rigid registration for 3D point clouds
  • A 3D point-cloud structurization algorithm
  • Distributional inference following minimax registration

I also collaborate with Vitruvian Building on point-cloud methods for concrete printing, geometric measurement, and quality assessment.

PUBLISHED WORK

Process and design relationships in 3D point clouds

My 2025 first-author paper in Manufacturing Letters uses unsupervised learning to study the relationship between manufacturing process and design embedded in 3D point-cloud data.

03

PROPOSED RESEARCH

GPU-accelerated geometric analysis

I plan to investigate whether GPU parallelism can accelerate 3D point-cloud registration and geometric analysis. I will profile the registration workflow, identify computational bottlenecks, and evaluate suitable computations on GPUs.

The evaluation will compare CPU and GPU runtime, memory use, registration accuracy, and stability across point-cloud sizes and measurement noise.

This work is proposed. No GPU speedup has been measured yet.

04

HEALTHCARE RESEARCH

Statistical learning for clinical EEG

With collaborators at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center, I am developing and evaluating statistical and machine learning methods for EEG artifact detection and interval-level detection of intraoperative cerebral ischemia.

My 2026 first-author paper studies a lightweight deep convolutional neural network for artifact detection in continuous EEG signals.
