RESEARCH

Research interests

Statistical and computational approaches to problems in manufacturing and applied engineering.

01

Advanced manufacturing and 3D point clouds

My manufacturing research includes 3D point-cloud processing, geometric measurement, point-cloud registration, reverse engineering, and additive manufacturing. I am interested in how geometric data can inform the analysis of manufactured components and support engineering decisions.

02

Statistics, machine learning, and optimization

My work draws on applied statistics, statistical learning, numerical optimization, and uncertainty quantification. These methods support the study of complex data and the development of reliable computational approaches.

03

Healthcare analytics

I also work on statistical and machine learning approaches to clinical electroencephalography (EEG), including artifact analysis and intraoperative neuromonitoring.

Research direction

I am interested in efficient computation for large geometric datasets, including opportunities for GPU-accelerated methods in 3D analysis and manufacturing inspection.
