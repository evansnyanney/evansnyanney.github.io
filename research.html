Research

Research Experience and Interests

My research focuses on 3D point-cloud registration, geometric uncertainty quantification, and manufacturing quality assessment. I am also developing statistical and machine learning methods for clinical EEG analysis.

Current Work

  1. 3D point-cloud registration and inference: I am developing statistical methods for point-cloud registration and geometric uncertainty characterization in reverse engineering and additive manufacturing. With Dr. Zhaohui Geng, I am preparing manuscripts on minimax rigid registration, a 3D point-cloud structurization algorithm, and distributional inference following minimax registration. View the project ↗
  2. Concrete printing and quality assessment: I am collaborating with Vitruvian Building on point-cloud methods for concrete printing, geometric measurement, and quality assessment.
  3. Statistical learning for clinical EEG: With collaborators at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center, I am developing and evaluating methods for EEG artifact detection and interval-level detection of intraoperative cerebral ischemia. My 2026 first-author paper studies a lightweight deep convolutional neural network for artifact detection in continuous EEG signals. View publications and preprints ↗

Published Work

  1. Process and design relationships in 3D point clouds: My 2025 first-author paper in Manufacturing Letters uses unsupervised learning to study the relationship between manufacturing process and design embedded in 3D point-cloud data. Read the paper ↗

Proposed Research

  1. GPU-accelerated geometric analysis: I plan to profile the registration workflow, identify computational bottlenecks, and test GPU parallelism for 3D point-cloud registration and geometric analysis. I will compare CPU and GPU runtime, memory use, registration accuracy, and stability across point-cloud sizes and measurement noise. This work is proposed, and no GPU speedup has been measured yet.

Methods and Tools

Applied statistics, statistical learning, numerical optimization, and uncertainty quantification. Python and PyTorch, with working knowledge of CUDA and GPU computing. View my academic CV ↗
