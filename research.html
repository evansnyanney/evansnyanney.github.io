Research

RESEARCH

Reliable geometry for manufacturing

Statistical methods for 3D registration, geometric uncertainty, and manufacturing quality assessment.

How can 3D measurements support reliable engineering decisions?

A point cloud captures the geometry of a measured object. My research asks how to align these measurements and characterize geometric uncertainty so that they can support reverse engineering and additive manufacturing inspection.

01

CURRENT RESEARCH

Registration and inference

I am developing statistical methods for 3D point-cloud registration and geometric uncertainty characterization. Ongoing manuscripts with Dr. Zhaohui Geng address minimax rigid registration, point-cloud structurization, and distributional inference following minimax registration. These manuscripts are in preparation.

My manufacturing work also includes collaboration with Vitruvian Building on point-cloud methods for concrete printing, geometric measurement, and quality assessment.

PROPOSED DIRECTION · ACCELERATED COMPUTING

Scaling geometric analysis with GPUs

I am interested in investigating whether GPU parallelism can accelerate the repeated geometric computations needed to align and analyze large point clouds. A proposed next step is to profile the registration workflow, identify its dominant costs, and evaluate suitable computations on GPUs.

My planned evaluation would compare CPU and GPU runtime, memory use, registration accuracy, and stability across point-cloud sizes and measurement noise. The goal is faster analysis with reliable geometric and statistical results. This is a future direction; no GPU speedup is reported here.
