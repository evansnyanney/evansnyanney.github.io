Hello!

My name is Evans. I am currently a PhD student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Ohio University, where I am advised by Dr. Zhaohui Geng. My research interests include 3D point-cloud analysis and uncertainty quantification for advanced manufacturing, including additive manufacturing, reverse engineering, and quality assessment. I also work on clinical EEG analysis and intraoperative neuromonitoring (IONM).

I grew up in Ghana, where I earned my bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Actuarial Science at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology. I first came to the United States in 2021 and later earned my master's degree in Mathematical Sciences (Applied Statistics and Data Science) at the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. Outside research, I enjoy playing ping pong (table tennis).
