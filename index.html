WELCOME TO MY ACADEMIC WEBSITE

Hello.

My name is Evans Nyanney. I am a PhD student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Ohio University, where I work on 3D point-cloud processing, geometric data analysis, and computational methods for advanced manufacturing.

My academic path began in mathematics and continued through applied statistics and data science. This background has shaped how I approach engineering problems, combining analytical reasoning with computation to better understand real-world measurements and systems.

I am interested in research that connects mathematical ideas with engineering applications, and I enjoy learning from collaborations across disciplines.
