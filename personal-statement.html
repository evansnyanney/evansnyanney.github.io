PERSONAL STATEMENT

My journey

The experiences and interests that have shaped my approach to research.

Welcome!

My academic journey began in mathematics, where I developed an interest in understanding complex problems through logical reasoning and careful analysis. As I continued my studies in applied statistics and data science, I became increasingly interested in how mathematical ideas could be used to address real-world challenges. This interest led me to industrial and systems engineering, where I discovered the opportunities that computational methods offer for understanding manufacturing processes and improving engineering decisions.

Through my research in 3D point-cloud processing and geometric data analysis, I have learned that solving engineering problems requires more than technical knowledge. It involves asking meaningful questions, exploring different approaches, and learning from both successes and challenges. These experiences have strengthened my analytical skills, encouraged me to think independently, and helped me appreciate the importance of collaboration in research.

This website reflects my academic journey and brings together the research, projects, and experiences that have shaped my development. As I continue my career, my goal is to contribute to research and education by developing practical methods that advance engineering knowledge and address important challenges in industry.

Evans Nyanney
