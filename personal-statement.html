Personal Statement

Welcome!

I am currently a PhD student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Ohio University. Before I entered this program, my academic training was in mathematics, applied statistics, and data science; through my doctoral studies, I have been applying this foundation to research problems in manufacturing and healthcare. Throughout my research, I have been involved in 3D point-cloud analysis, registration and geometric uncertainty characterization, and clinical EEG analysis. These activities include work on reverse engineering, additive manufacturing quality assessment, and intraoperative neuromonitoring. As I take part in each research activity, I am developing my strengths in the following areas: finding relevant research to support the development of methods, analyzing possible approaches in relation to the problem and available data, and beginning each investigation with a well-defined objective.

This portfolio provides an insight into the research process I am developing through my doctoral studies and presents some of the research activities I have participated in and their relation to my interests in statistical and computational engineering. The publications and projects included here provide concrete examples of this work, from the study of manufacturing geometry to the analysis of clinical signals. My goal for future development is that as I continue my career in engineering, I will improve my ability to develop and evaluate new approaches to research problems, strengthen my computational skills, and find better ways to seek and use the input of collaborators in the research process.

— Evans Nyanney
