Personal Statement

Welcome!

My background in mathematics, applied statistics, and data science has shaped the way I approach engineering problems. Through my research, I have been applying this foundation to 3D point-cloud analysis, geometric uncertainty characterization, and clinical EEG analysis. These activities have included work on reverse engineering, additive manufacturing quality assessment, and intraoperative neuromonitoring. As I take part in each research activity, I am strengthening my ability to find relevant research to support and generate ideas, analyze possible approaches against the objectives and constraints of a problem, and begin each investigation with a clearly defined question.

This portfolio provides an insight into the research process I am developing and brings together examples of the activities that have shaped my approach to engineering problems. The publications and projects presented here illustrate my work in manufacturing geometry, statistical learning, and clinical signal analysis. My goal for future development is that as I continue my career in engineering, I will improve my ability to generate and evaluate new ideas, develop reliable computational methods for complex data, and find better ways to seek and use the input of collaborators in the research process.

— Evans Nyanney
