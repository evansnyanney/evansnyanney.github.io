Personal Statement

Welcome!

I am currently a PhD student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Ohio University, where I am advised by Dr. Zhaohui Geng. Before entering this program, I studied mathematics, applied statistics, and data science, which provided the foundation for my interest in computational approaches to engineering problems. Through my doctoral research, I have been working on 3D point-cloud registration and geometric uncertainty characterization for reverse engineering and additive manufacturing quality assessment. I have also been involved in collaborative work on concrete printing and clinical EEG analysis, including artifact detection and intraoperative neuromonitoring. These research activities bring together several aspects of the approach I am developing: defining a clear research question, using statistical reasoning to understand the data, and evaluating computational methods in relation to the application they are intended to support.

This website provides an insight into my development as a researcher and brings together the publications and projects that reflect my interests in manufacturing, statistical learning, and computational engineering. The work presented here includes published research as well as ongoing investigations into point-cloud registration and geometric analysis. As I continue my doctoral studies, my goal is to strengthen my ability to develop reliable methods for engineering data, investigate how GPU computing can support the analysis of large geometric datasets, and learn from collaborators working on manufacturing and clinical applications. I also hope to build on my teaching experience as I prepare for a career in research and education.

— Evans Nyanney
